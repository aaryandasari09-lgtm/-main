How a Website Works (Plain English)

Think of a website like a shop. There are three things you need:

🏗️
The Building

The actual web files (HTML, CSS) — the pages, design, and content. This is what we've already built.

🌍
The Address

The domain name (e.g. goldentouchhair.com.au) — how people find them online.

🖥️
The Land

Hosting — a computer (server) that stores the files and makes them available 24/7.

When someone types goldentouchhair.com.au into their browser, it does this:

👤 User types URL → DNS looks up the address → Finds the hosting server → Downloads the HTML/CSS files → ✓ Website displays in browser

The key point to remember

We've built the building. We still need to set up the address (domain) and the land (hosting) before the world can see it. That part is simple and is covered in Section 3.

What are the files actually made of?

The website has 3 types of files — you don't need to know how to write them, just understand what they do:

  • 📄 .html — The content. Text, headings, buttons, structure. Like a Word document.
  • 🎨 .css — The design. Colours, fonts, layout, animations.
  • ⚡ .js — The behaviour. Things that move or respond (like the hamburger menu).

Domain & Hosting Explained

Domain Name

A domain is the web address — e.g. goldentouchhair.com.au. You rent it (usually annually). For an Australian business, .com.au is the most trusted extension. Cost: ~$15–$25/year.

Where to buy a domain

Use Namecheap (namecheap.com), Crazy Domains (crazydomain.com.au — Australian, popular for .com.au), or VentraIP (Australian registrar). Search for the name, add to cart, pay. Done in 5 minutes.

Hosting

Hosting is a server (a computer that never turns off) that stores the website files and shows them to visitors. You pay monthly or yearly to "rent space" on it.

For this website, the best option is Netlify — it's free for basic static sites like this one and is trusted by millions of developers worldwide.

Why Netlify is perfect for this

Free plan covers everything this website needs. Fast. No server management. Custom domain connection is free. SSL certificate (the padlock 🔒) is automatic and free. Takes 10 minutes to set up.

What is SSL / the padlock 🔒?

SSL is the security certificate that makes the URL show https:// instead of http://. It means the connection is encrypted and safe. Google penalises sites without it. Netlify gives it free automatically when you connect a domain.

What is DNS?

DNS (Domain Name System) is like a phonebook. When you register a domain (e.g. at Crazy Domains) and host it on Netlify, you need to tell the domain where to point. You do this by updating the domain's "nameservers" — Netlify gives you two lines to paste in, and it's done in under 5 minutes. Changes take 24–48 hours to fully activate (called "propagation").

How to Publish This Website — Step by Step

This is the actual process from "files on your laptop" to "live on the internet". You can do all of this yourself — it takes about 30 minutes total.

What you need before starting

The golden-touch-website folder (downloaded from GitHub) · A free Netlify account (sign up at netlify.com) · A domain purchased from Crazy Domains or Namecheap · A credit card for the domain (~$20)

Step A — Deploy to Netlify (free, takes 2 minutes)

  1. 1

    Go to app.netlify.com/drop

    You don't even need an account for this step. Just go to the URL in your browser.

  2. 2

    Drag the golden-touch-website folder onto the page

    The whole folder — not just one file. Netlify uploads all 5 files at once.

  3. 3

    You get an instant live link

    Something like https://funny-name-123456.netlify.app. The site is now live. Share this for the preview.

  4. 4

    Create a free Netlify account and claim the site

    Sign up, then "claim" the site you just deployed. This lets you manage it, update files, and connect a custom domain.

Step B — Buy a Domain (~$20, takes 5 minutes)

  1. 1

    Go to crazydomains.com.au or namecheap.com

    Search for goldentouchhair.com.au or goldentouchhairstyle.com.au

  2. 2

    Purchase it for 1–2 years

    Pay with card. ~$15–25/year for a .com.au. You'll get login credentials to the registrar dashboard.

Step C — Connect the Domain to Netlify (takes 10 minutes + 24 hours to activate)

  1. 1

    In Netlify: go to Site Settings → Domain Management → Add Custom Domain

    Type in the domain you just bought (e.g. goldentouchhair.com.au)

  2. 2

    Netlify shows you 2 nameserver addresses

    They look like: dns1.p05.nsone.net and dns2.p05.nsone.net

  3. 3

    Log in to your domain registrar and update the nameservers

    Go to DNS settings for the domain → replace the existing nameservers with the two Netlify ones → save.

  4. 4

    Wait 24–48 hours for the change to propagate globally

    After that, visiting goldentouchhair.com.au will show the Golden Touch website. Netlify automatically adds the free SSL padlock.

✓ That's it — the website is live

Anyone in the world who types the domain will see the Golden Touch Hair Style website. No ongoing technical work needed. If content needs updating, you re-drag the updated folder to Netlify — takes 30 seconds.

Key Terms Glossary

DomainThe web address (e.g. goldentouchhair.com.au). Rented annually from a registrar like Crazy Domains.
HostingA server that stores the website files and serves them to visitors 24/7. Netlify is our hosting provider (free tier).
DNSDomain Name System — like a phonebook that connects a domain name to a server. You update "nameservers" to point a domain to Netlify.
SSL / HTTPSThe security certificate that adds the 🔒 padlock. Makes the URL start with https://. Netlify provides this for free.
HTMLThe content and structure of a web page — text, buttons, images, links.
CSSThe design/styling — colours, fonts, layouts, animations.
ResponsiveA website that automatically adjusts its layout to look good on mobile, tablet, and desktop.
SEOSearch Engine Optimisation — the practice of making a website appear higher in Google search results. Local SEO targets searches like "barber near me".
NetlifyThe hosting platform we're using. Free for static websites. You drag a folder in and it's live. Trusted by millions of developers.
PropagationThe 24–48 hour period after connecting a domain where the change spreads globally across all DNS servers. Normal and expected.
Google Business ProfileThe free Google listing that shows your business on Google Maps and in local search results. Adding the website URL to it helps Google rank the site faster.
Static websiteA website made of plain HTML/CSS files — no database, no CMS. Fast, cheap to host (often free), simple, secure. Perfect for a barbershop site.
CMSContent Management System (e.g. WordPress) — lets non-technical users edit content. Not needed for this site; updates are simple text file edits.
RegistrarThe company you buy a domain from (Crazy Domains, Namecheap, VentraIP). Separate from the hosting company.

How Contact & Booking Works After They Own the Website

This is a critical thing to understand — and to be able to explain clearly. A static website doesn't automatically "receive" form submissions or phone calls. Here's exactly how the pipeline works:

The 3 Ways Clients Contact Them

  1. 1

    Phone Call (most immediate)

    The "Call Us Now" button in the hero and CTA sections uses a tel: link. On a phone, tapping it auto-dials. Once you add the real phone number, this works immediately — no setup needed. This is still how most people book a barbershop.

  2. 2

    Booking Form → Netlify Forms (automatic, free)

    The contact form has a data-netlify="true" attribute already added. When hosted on Netlify, this one attribute makes Netlify automatically capture every form submission — no backend, no server, no code needed. Submissions appear in the Netlify dashboard AND get emailed to the business. Free up to 100 submissions/month.

  3. 3

    "Book Now" → Links to Fresha

    The main "Book Now" buttons can link directly to their Fresha booking page (just replace the href in the button). Clients click Book Now → land on Fresha → pick a time → confirmed. The website drives the traffic; Fresha handles the scheduling.

How Netlify Forms Works (Step by Step)

What the business owner needs to know

They log into their free Netlify account → click "Forms" in the dashboard → see every booking request submitted with all the details filled in. They can also set up email notifications so every submission goes straight to their inbox or phone.

  1. 1

    Client fills in the form on the website

    Name, phone, preferred location, service, date/time, any notes. Hits "Request Appointment."

  2. 2

    Netlify captures the submission instantly

    Because the form has data-netlify="true". No backend code needed — Netlify handles it automatically.

  3. 3

    Business gets an email notification

    Configure in Netlify dashboard: Settings → Forms → Notifications → add their email. Every submission triggers an email with all the details.

  4. 4

    Business calls/texts the client to confirm

    They see the client's phone number in the submission — quick call to confirm the slot. Takes 1 minute.

What the Business Actually "Does" Day to Day

Once the site is live, the day-to-day is simple:

  • ✓ Check their Netlify email notifications for new booking requests
  • ✓ Call/text clients back to confirm times
  • ✓ Log into Netlify dashboard to see all submissions
  • ✓ Keep Fresha updated for existing regular clients
  • — No technical knowledge required once it's live

💬 How to explain this in the meeting

"You don't need to do anything technical. Someone fills in the form, you get an email on your phone with their name, number, what they want, and when. You call them back to confirm. That's it. Or if you prefer, we link the Book Now button straight to your Fresha — completely up to you."
