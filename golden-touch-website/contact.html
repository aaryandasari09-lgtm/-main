FAQs

Common Questions

Do I need to book in advance? +

Walk-ins are always welcome and we do our best to fit you in. However, we recommend booking ahead for Saturdays and Thursday late nights, as these are our busiest times. Online booking is available 24/7 through this page.

How long does a typical appointment take? +

A standard men's haircut takes 30–45 minutes. A fade takes 40–55 minutes. Beard trims are 20–30 minutes. Our VIP package runs 80–100 minutes. Exact times can vary based on hair length and complexity.

Do you cut kids' hair? +

Absolutely! Kids' haircuts start from $25 and our barbers are experienced at making young clients feel relaxed and comfortable. We welcome boys of all ages, including first-timers.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept cash, card (EFTPOS / tap), and most digital payment options. There are no surcharges for card payments.

Is parking available? +

Yes — there is ample free parking directly outside our shop at Lakeside Parade, Jordan Springs. We're easy to find and accessible for everyone.
