The Problem

Why Fresha Alone Is Holding You Back

Fresha is a useful booking tool — but it's a platform they own and control. Here's what it can't do for Golden Touch:

🔍

It won't help you rank on Google

When someone types "barber Jordan Springs" into Google, your Fresha listing almost never appears on the first page. A proper website with your suburb name, services, and reviews gets you ranked where clients are actually searching.

🏷️

You're listed alongside your competitors

On Fresha, clients browsing for a barber see Golden Touch right next to every other barbershop. A website means they come directly to you — no comparison shopping.

🎨

Zero brand control

Fresha dictates the layout, the colours, the fonts. You can't show your personality, your story, or what makes Golden Touch different. A website is 100% yours — it looks exactly how you want.

⭐

Your great reviews are buried

Golden Touch has fantastic reviews. On Fresha, they're hidden behind a few clicks. A website puts your best reviews front and centre — the first thing every visitor sees.

📵

No way to share your link professionally

Sending someone your Fresha link looks amateur. A real website URL — goldentouchhair.com.au — looks professional on business cards, Instagram bio, Google Business, and word of mouth.

💸

Fresha can change its rules (or fees) at any time

You don't own your Fresha page. If they introduce fees, change the algorithm, or go down — you lose your online presence overnight. A website is an asset you own forever.
