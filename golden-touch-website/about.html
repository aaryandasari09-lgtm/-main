Who We Are

Golden Touch Hair Style

Nestled in the vibrant community of Jordan Springs at 11/56 Lakeside Parade, Golden Touch Hair Style was founded with a single ambition: to bring genuine barbering craft to the Penrith region — and to make every man feel confident when he walks out our door.

What started as a neighbourhood barbershop quickly became a destination. Word spread fast — not just about the quality of our fades and beard trims, but about the experience. The welcoming atmosphere. The barbers who actually listen. The consistency that keeps clients coming back week after week.

Today, Golden Touch Hair Style is proudly open 7 days a week, serving the men and families of Jordan Springs, Penrith, St Clair, and beyond. Our commitment to the craft — and to our community — hasn't changed one bit.
